Quick summary Freshdesk has three main plans: Growth ($19/agent/month), Pro ($55), and Enterprise ($89), all billed annually. They don’t have a free tier anymore. Their AI features are sold separately: Most teams end up upgrading when they need SLA management, skill-based routing, or the Copilot AI, since those are locked behind the pricier plans.
